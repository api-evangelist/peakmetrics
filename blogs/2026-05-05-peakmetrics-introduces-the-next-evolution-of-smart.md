Los Angeles, CA (May 5th)— PeakMetrics, the leader in AI-driven narrative intelligence, today introduced the next evolution of Smart Categories, a breakthrough in how organizations understand and act on online conversations. Smart Categories give teams a clearer, more structured view of the online landscape by using AI to organize data into the themes, tones, and signals that actually matter. Instead of sifting through noise, teams can instantly see what is happening, why it is happening, and who is driving the conversation.
