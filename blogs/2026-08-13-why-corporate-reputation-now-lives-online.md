Corporate reputation now lives where people scroll, not where press releases sit. During hot summer months, when politics, money worries, and culture wars are all flaring at once, a single post can spark a storm in hours. For communication and risk teams, that means the real action is happening in comment threads, stitched videos, and late-night forum posts.
