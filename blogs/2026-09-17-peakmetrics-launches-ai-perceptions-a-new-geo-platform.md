Los Angeles — September 17th, 2026— PeakMetrics, the AI-native Narrative Intelligence company, today announced the launch of AI Perceptions , a new Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) solution built specifically for communications teams to understand and improve how their brands are represented across leading AI platforms. As consumers, journalists, investors, and other stakeholders increasingly turn to ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, and Perplexity for information, AI-generated answers are becoming an important part of brand reputation. But those answers do not exist in isolation.
