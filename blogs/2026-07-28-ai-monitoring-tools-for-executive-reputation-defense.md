Leaders can go from calm to crisis in a single news cycle. A short clip, a leaked email, or a half-true claim can spin across social platforms, fringe forums, and news before anyone has time to breathe. When that happens, executives do not just need more data; they need fast, clear insight into what is being said and how it is spreading.
