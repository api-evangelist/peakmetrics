Following the tragic passing of actress Hayden Panettiere , people online are revisiting an account she shared earlier this year about her former relationship with Neutrogena—and raising new questions about how brands support their longtime partners during deeply personal moments. Panettiere represented Neutrogena for roughly a decade. Earlier this year, she alleged that the company sought to end her endorsement after she publicly discussed experiencing postpartum depression in 2015 while promoting the television series Nashville .
