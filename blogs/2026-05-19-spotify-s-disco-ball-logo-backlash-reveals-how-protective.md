Another brand logo is back in the crosshairs. Just months after the Cracker Barrel branding conversations sparked debate online, Spotify found itself at the center of its own internet-wide backlash after temporarily swapping its iconic green app logo for a glittery disco ball version tied to the company’s 20th anniversary campaign. The move was meant to be celebratory.
